## Design Context

### Users
Students, professors and evaluators of the UJAP Servicio Comunitario (Escuela de Arquitectura), plus parish/archdiocese staff. They reach the site from a link or by scanning a printed QR code (often on a phone, on site in a church), to find and read inventory PDFs of movable cultural heritage of the Arquidiócesis de Valencia (Venezuela).

### Brand Personality
Archival, devotional, scholarly. It should feel like a museum collection register or a catalog card drawer — serious and cared-for, not corporate and not "app-like". Emotional goal: trust and respect for the heritage being documented.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme (daytime reading, printed QR labels). Whitewashed limestone neutrals (from the Catedral de Valencia façade) tinted warm; UJAP red as the rare accent, UJAP gold for rules and numerals, UJAP blue only for focus. Editorial serif display (Brygada 1918) + clean grotesk body (Schibsted Grotesk). Documents presented as a numbered ledger (Nº 001…), hairline rules, no card grids. Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, purple gradients.

### Design Principles
1. The catalog is the hero: finding and opening a document must take one tap.
2. Register, not cards: numbered rows with hairline rules, like an inventory ledger.
3. Accent is rare: red only for primary actions and the active state.
4. Every QR prints well: high contrast, error-correction H, UJAP logo in the center.
5. Mobile-first in practice: people arrive from QR codes on phones.
